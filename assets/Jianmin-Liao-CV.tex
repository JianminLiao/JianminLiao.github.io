\section{Research Experience \& Publications}

\begin{twocolentry}{Jan 2026--Present}
  \textbf{\hrefWithoutArrow{https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.20479}{\textcolor{bodyColor}{Oracle Supervision Transfers for Hyperparameter Prediction in Model-Based Image Denoising}}}\\
  \textit{\color{mutedColor}First and corresponding author; NeurIPS 2026 Main Track (accepted)}
\end{twocolentry}
\begin{onecolentry}
\begin{highlights}
  \item Conceived HyperDn, designed the method and experiments, and wrote the manuscript; the configuration-conditioned PyTorch predictor uses a 2.18M-parameter ConvNeXtV2 backbone and transfers TV/TGV supervision from 13 source configurations to diffusion-based DiffPIR.
  \item With \textbf{2 DiffPIR target oracle labels}, achieved \textbf{30.23 dB}---within 0.90 dB of oracle and 2.50 dB above the identical architecture without source pretraining on WED $256\times256$.
  \item Without target oracle labels, transferred to two mixtures of individually seen noise types and three evaluated resolutions ($321\times481$, $512\times768$, $\sim\!1024\times768$), with 0.01--0.27 dB gaps to oracle.
  \item For L2-TV Gaussian, selected hyperparameters in 14--47 ms versus 2.04--10.86 s for Monte Carlo SURE on three OOD datasets, excluding the shared downstream solver.
  \item Extended transfer to SSIM and LPIPS objectives: with metric-specific training, one target label outperformed a from-scratch baseline using 16 labels on both metrics.
  \item Transferred to unseen NLM and CBM3D denoisers with different hyperparameter semantics, achieving the best mean PSNR among evaluated baselines at all seven tested few-shot budgets.
\end{highlights}
\end{onecolentry}
\vspace{0.11cm}

\begin{twocolentry}{Mar 2025--Jul 2026}
  \textbf{\hrefWithoutArrow{https://doi.org/10.1007/s10851-026-01329-2}{\textcolor{bodyColor}{MG-SpaIR: Multi-Grade Sparse-Guided Implicit Representation for Training-Data-Free Image Restoration}}}\\
  \textit{\color{mutedColor}Co-first and corresponding author; J. Math. Imaging Vis. 68, Article 48 (2026)}
\end{twocolentry}
\begin{onecolentry}
\begin{highlights}
  \item Co-developed a PyTorch framework that restores images from a single observation without an external training set under combined blur, downsampling, noise, and missing pixels.
  \item On the paper's mixed-degradation evaluation, achieved the highest average PSNR/SSIM among compared training-data-free approaches; multi-grade training used \textbf{30\% less VRAM} than single-grade.
  \item Across SIREN, WIRE, and Instant-NGP, validated the regularizer and established per-grade objective convergence and asymptotic stationarity under explicit regularity and step-size conditions.
\end{highlights}
\end{onecolentry}
