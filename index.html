01 / About

Learning to restore.
Learning with less.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Mathematics at Syracuse University, with a statistics emphasis. I develop data-efficient machine learning methods for image restoration and inverse problems.

My work connects computer vision, computational imaging, and numerical optimization—from transferring supervision across image denoisers to restoring images without an external training set.

Recent workMy first-author paper on HyperDn has been accepted to NeurIPS 2026, Main Track.
